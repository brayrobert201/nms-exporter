# Which substances can a mineral hotspot yield on each planet type? Can Ammonia be one?

## Why it matters

Whether a hands-off Ammonia (and other alloy input) farm exists.

## Known so far

Mineral1-3 hotspots draw from the planet's own substances; nothing about hotspots is stored in the save. Ammonia is a Toxic-planet substance. Game data (build 25800958) lists Ammonia (TOXIC1) among the 33 substances a hotspot can be extracted for, alongside Paraffinium, Pyrite, Uranium, Dioxite, Phosphorus, Faecium and Mordite. Still unknown: how a planet's Mineral1-3 hotspots pick from it, i.e. whether a Toxic planet reliably offers an Ammonia hotspot.

## What would answer it

A game-data citation (`scripts/nms-pak`) for the values, plus a measurement or code evidence for the behaviour.
