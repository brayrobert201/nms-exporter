Harvest All Plants From Freighter V5.54.2

 March 24, 2025March 23, 2025 - Leave a Comment 

This mod increases the harvest all radius of plants on freighters to cover the entire freighter. Clicking harvest all on one planter will harvest all of the plants in the freighter instead of having to tediously run through rows upon rows of plants and manually harvest them all.

To install, download the mod and extract it into Steam\steamapps\common\No Man’s Sky\GAMEDATA\MODS.
