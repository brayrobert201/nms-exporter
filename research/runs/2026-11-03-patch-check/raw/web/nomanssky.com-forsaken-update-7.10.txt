Meet strange traders, collect haunting mementos, deliver mysterious packages, construct an eerie corvette, jump between star systems with seamless warping and more in update 7.10, FORSAKEN! Introducing new spooky gameplay and bringing the full experience of No Man's Sky to iOS devices.

                A Collector of Unique Pieces
        Forsaken traders have appeared at the edges of deep-space outposts, seeking mementos to add to their strange collections. These enigmatic aliens stock a unique range of corvette modules, which they are willing to exchange for an appropriate offering…
                Minimal HUD Mode
        Immerse yourself deeper in the spectacle of the universe with a new minimal HUD mode. This setting is especially suited for experienced Travellers, minimising the compass, hazard protection bars, and mission notifications, and hiding non-essential markers.
                IOS Support
        Carry an infinite universe in your pocket! The full experience of No Man's Sky is now available on iOS devices. The user interface has been built for touch from the ground up, and the iOS engine has been optimised for battery life and thermodynamics, allowing for long, comfortable play sessions on mobile.
                Haunting Mementos
        Bizarre, horrifying biological samples have begun to appear in the spookiest regions of the universe. Search deep-space hulk wrecks, infested outposts, derelict freighters and palaeontological digsites, and harvest the haunting mementos found within. Offer these curios to forsaken traders in exchange for unique corvette modules, but beware the appearance of hostile boundary horrors…
                Vantage-Class Cockpit
        The ultra-wide Vantage-Class cockpit provides a broad, uninterrupted view of space, and a minimalist modern interior - for the corvette pilot with their eyes on the stars.
                Larva Tank Helmet
        Substitute your cranium with a parasitic larva, wriggling and writhing in its aquatic tank. Travellers who dedicate themselves to the harvest of haunting mementos can claim this exclusive exosuit appearance override, alongside the title of Mind Harvester.
                Space Courier Contracts
        Become a long-distance space trucker with deep-space delivery missions, available from space outposts. Clients will pay generously for the safe interplanetary delivery of their most important packages.
                Sinister Corvette Modules
        Design the biomechanical corvette of your nightmares - or add a touch of the sinister to your existing corvette builds - with a huge array of new modules available from forsaken traders.

                Hornet-Class Cockpit
        The segmented panels of the Hornet-Class cockpit lend an insectoid quality to corvette builds. Select one of three variants to perfect your hornet-headed design.
                Legion-Class Interiors
        Outfit your corvette with the cruiser-style Legion-class habitation modules. Narrow ceilings, recessed illumination and stark angles bring a utilitarian aesthetic to corvette interiors.
                Larva Cannon
        Surrender an offering of preserved grubs to a forsaken trader, and acquire the grotesque Larva Cannon Multi-Tool, fashioned from a larval exoskeleton of unknown provenance.
                Viper-Class Cockpit
        Underlit, leather-bound panels and a gridded viewport define the Viper-class cockpit. Prime your ship weapons and strike out at foes with this serpentine head for corvettes, available in three structural variants.
                Radial Weapon Selector
        Travellers with a decked-out Multi-Tool can now switch efficiently between weapons and utilities using the new weapon select wheel, without needing to cycle through each mode individually.
                Uploadable Space Bases
        Off-planet bases may now be uploaded to the shared No Man’s Sky server, allowing other Travellers to visit and admire your orbital constructions.
                Mantis-Class Cockpit
        Square-cornered panels and reinforced titanium bars frame your view from the Mantis-class cockpit. Personalise your build by configuring the surrounding flush vents and fuselage into one of three varieties.
                Seamless Warping
        Stars from nearby systems now appear as bright, targetable destinations in deep space - and can be directly flown to, bypassing the requirement to navigate via the Galaxy Map.
                Space Scavenging Guidance
        Detailed guidance is available for deep-space scavengers. Jump into your corvette, equip a Gravitino Coil, and strip deep-space hulks and asteroid belts for precious technologies and minerals.
                Deep-Space Creature Battles
        Holo-Arena battles can now be fought at deep-space outposts. Assemble your strongest creature companions into a battle team and face off against these new challengers!

            7.10 Patch notes
            Forsaken Traders
- A variety of haunting mementos have manifested around the galaxy; appearing at infested outposts, deep-space hulks, derelict freighters, and buried between the bones at planetary digsites.
- Travellers will need to apply caution when harvesting these mementos, as prolonged exposure will attract the attention of hostile boundary horrors.
- Drawn to these collectable curios, forsaken traders can now be summoned at space outposts. Trade mementos to these enigmatic shopkeeps in exchange for a range of new spooky corvette modules, including cockpits, habs, and biomechanical hull structures.
- Forsaken traders also stock a living Larva Cannon Multi-Tool, purchasable for the right quantity of preserved grubs.
- Determined salvagers can earn a unique player title and Larva Tank helmet customisation.
            Minimal HUD Mode
- In-game Heads Up Display (HUD) elements, such as the compass, hazard protection, and mission notifications, can now be set to "Minimal" mode, reducing visual noise for experienced players.
            Space Courier Missions
- Travellers with a corvette and gravitino coil can now undertake package courier missions from deep-space outposts.
            Seamless Warping
- Stars from nearby star systems can now be directly targeted and warped to from space, without opening the Galaxy Map.
- If the current mission target is interstellar, the nearby star will be indicated with the mission icon.
            Multi-Tool Mode Wheel
- Multi-Tool modes can now be selected via a radial menu.
            Space Holo-Arenas
- A Holo-Arena challenger has appeared at deep-space outposts, with a unique battle configuration.
            Quality of Life
- Orbital constructions in deep space can now be uploaded to the server.
- Improved the mission guidance for retrieving materials from asteroid belts and hulks with a corvette and gravitino coil.
- Deep-space materials added in the Cosmos update can now be pinned in the catalogue, for additional guidance in locating them.
- Added the exterior space station decoration parts to the Catalogue.
- Added a corvette landing pad to planetary trading outposts.
- The Oxygen Harvester may now be constructed anywhere on a planetary surface, not just within a base.
- Improved the layout of the technology research trees at the Construction Research Station.
- Improved the text for a number of space station mission agent missions.
- Improved the variety of descriptions for points of interest on the solar system map.
- Improved the variety of mission names at the deep-space salvage & delivery contracts terminal.
